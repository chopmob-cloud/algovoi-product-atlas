## The two access models

The estate is consumed two ways, and almost every page assumes one of them:

- **Governed (hosted).** Tenant, API key, KYB. The operator carries the compliance duty; the platform carries the state.
- **Proof (tenant-free).** No account, no key. Settlement is the credential and the receipt is the evidence.

Self-hosting is the third path and is not a host at all: the same products run under a free perpetual licence. See the subject index → *Self-host and operations*.
